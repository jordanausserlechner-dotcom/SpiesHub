SPIESHUB VERDICT

Built to carry.

The RUSH 24 2.0 sits in an interesting middle ground. At 37 litres, it's considerably larger than a typical everyday backpack but doesn't move into oversized expedition-pack territory. That makes it a versatile option for people who want one bag that can cover work, travel, outdoor use and heavier everyday carry.

Its strengths are organisation, durability and modularity. The trade-off is that it is a substantial pack, and its tactical styling won't appeal to everyone.

PROS & CONSIDERATIONS

The good & the trade-offs.

What we like

  • 37L capacity gives plenty of usable space
  • Strong organisation throughout the pack
  • Padded laptop sleeve for up to 15" laptop
  • MOLLE attachment points provide modularity
  • Water-repellent 1050D nylon construction
  • Hydration compartment

Things to consider

  • 37L can be more pack than some everyday users need
  • Tactical appearance isn't for everyone
  • Multiple compartments can add complexity
  • Weight is higher than a minimalist daypack

WHO IS IT FOR?

Where it fits.

Good choice for

People who want a durable, highly organised backpack for work, travel, outdoor use, photography or carrying a larger amount of equipment.

Look elsewhere if...

You mainly want an ultra-light minimalist daypack, or you prefer a discreet conventional-looking backpack.
